# 0012: Spaces, pages and who can open them

- **Status:** accepted (2026-10-27). Core lane, page model. Names are in [0007](0007-shared-contracts.md) (sections 3.3 and 4.1); the rules come from the Sidebar, spaces and nested pages spec, the Pages and blocks spec and the Roles and permissions spec (RP-1 to RP-26).
- **Context:** Pages, databases and rows share one tree. Routes, live co-editing, comments, search, notices and the other lanes all need the same answer to "what may this person do here?", and none of them may show a title or content to someone without access.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Tables** (`*_pages_spaces_access.sql`): `spaces`, `space_members`, `pages` (0007 section 4.1, plus `cover_position`, `style`, `locked_*`, `archived_*` and `trash_root_id`), `access_grants` and `access_history`. A page's parent is always in the same space (a composite foreign key), and deleting a page for good deletes everything under it.
  2. **Spaces** (Sidebar spec 3.3): `kind` is `open`, `closed` or `private` for team spaces, or `personal` for each person's own Private section (one per person and workspace; `getPrivateSpaceId` returns it). Team spaces have members and owners (people or groups, never guests), a default access for members (`edit` unless changed), the owners' switches, a default-space flag ("General" in a new workspace) and archive and Trash dates. The order of spaces is per person, so spaces have no shared position.
  3. **Levels:** `none < view < comment < edit_content < edit < full`. A grant may also be `rows_only`. `edit_content` and `rows_only` are given only on databases.
  4. **A person's level on a page**, worked out in one query for any number of pages:
     - **Private section:** its owner has `full`; nobody else has anything, admins included (RP-D1), unless a page in it is shared with them.
     - **Team space:** Admins have `full`. Everyone else gets the highest of what reaches the page: grants on the page itself, then on each parent while the page below still inherits, and, when the top page inherits, the space's own grants plus the space's default access for its members (`full` for its owners). A grant reaches a person directly, through their role, through a group, or as everyone in the workspace.
     - **Below a database**, `edit_content` counts as `edit` and `rows_only` as `none`; on the database itself `rows_only` is `view`. Then the Databases lane's `row` resolver may change a row's level; its `ctx.grantLevel` is the best grant that reached the database, as given, so it can open the rows someone is in. `getGrantLevel(db, userId, pageId)` gives the same level as `getPageAccess`, except `rows_only` where only such a grant opens the database.
     - **Last, the role limit** from 0007 section 3.3, and pages in an archived space are read-only (`view` at most). Guests (`kind = 'guest'`) are reached only by grants to them or to their groups, never by role or everyone grants or space membership; their grants decide without a role limit, up to `edit`.
  5. **Changing access:** `grantAccess` and `revokeAccess` need `full` where the grant is, which also means nobody grants above their own level. Guests never get `full`, and sharing with a guest also needs the Add guests permission (`guests.add`) and a space that allows guests (`allow_guests`); group grants are refused until groups exist; grants on spaces come with the spaces API. Every change adds an `access_history` row in the same transaction.
  6. **Answers:** 404 "This was deleted, or the link is wrong." for an ID that is missing, malformed or in Trash; 403 "You don't have access to this." for anything else that's closed, never with a title (access requests start from the 403). `app.requirePageAccess(min, pageIdOf)` answers this way (a route that lets guests in with `config: { allowGuests: true }` must check the page grant itself, with it or `getPageAccess`), and `pageAccessOf(request)` gives the route the level it found.
  7. **Registration:** `features/pages/routes.ts` is wrapped in `fastify-plugin`, so `app.requirePageAccess` works in every feature registered after it. A prefix wouldn't apply to it, so its routes use their full paths (`/api/spaces`, `/api/pages`, `/api/shared`, `/api/trash`, `/api/favorites`, `/api/recent`, `/api/me/prefs`) and its line in `app.ts` has no prefix.
  8. **Events:** service functions (`createPage`, `changePageKind`, `trashPage`, `grantAccess` and the others) never emit, because only the caller knows when its transaction commits. The page model's routes emit `page.*` and `access.changed` after their commit; a feature that calls these functions in its own transaction emits after its own commit.
  9. **Nothing stored is shown without a check** (issue #14): pages store other pages only by ID, titles are looked up for the person viewing, and every list (tree, Shared, Trash, favorites, recent, cards, previews) is filtered with `getAccessForPages` or the same query when it's read. Stored copies such as Markdown or search text are only served after the same check.
  10. **Leaving:** someone removed from the workspace (`workspace_members.removed_at`) has no access anywhere, grants and space memberships included, until they're added back. Pages in a space that's in Trash count as in Trash (404).
  11. **Managing spaces** (pages 2): owners and Admins change a space's settings and members; only Admins mark default spaces and unarchive (SB-09). An archived space takes no changes until it's unarchived. A Private space answers 404 to everyone but its members and Admins, so nobody else learns it exists. People join Open spaces by themselves; Closed ones need an owner (join requests come next). Members leave Open and Closed spaces by themselves, and the last owner hands over first. Guests have no spaces. Deleting a space moves it, with its pages, to Trash: they answer 404 and the name is free again (restoring comes with Trash).
  12. **General and default spaces:** at start-up a workspace without any team space gets the Open default space "General" (SB-01), which has no owners, so Admins manage it. `joinDefaultSpaces` puts a new member in the default spaces; it runs on `member.added` once accounts emits that event, and the seed calls it until then. `setUpWorkspaceSpaces` returns the seed's team space.
  13. **Pages** (pages 3): a new page needs the Create permission and Can edit where it goes: under a page that isn't locked, at the top of a team space the person is in (owners only, if the space says so, and never while it's archived), or in their own Private section. Guests have no Create permission but may add sub-pages where they can edit (never at the top). `createPage` gives the creator a person grant of `full` (`edit` for a guest), still limited by the role, so the creator keeps the page after Stop inheriting or a move; it isn't a sharing change, so access history leaves it out. Rows are made only through `createPage`, by the Databases lane. A locked page refuses every change except unlocking (403) until someone with Can edit unlocks it. Breadcrumbs and cards never name a page the person can't open: each run of closed parents becomes one `{ hidden: true }`, and a card is `locked`, or `deleted` when the page is gone or in Trash and the person could open it. A new page's parent is checked for Can edit (404 when it's gone or in Trash, 403 when it's closed) before anything else about it is told. Pages nest at most 100 levels deep, top level included (`MAX_DEPTH`); a create or move that would go deeper is refused (400), so the tree and the access walks stay well inside their 1,000-level caps. Page JSON sends `parentId: null` when the person can't open the parent, and a Private space the person isn't in (not a member, not an Admin) as "Private space" with no icon; cards leave its name out (point 11). Uploaded covers (`type: 'file'`) are refused (400) until file uploads land with their checks (a picture on a page the person may use). For the Meetings lane: `createPage` takes `inheritAccess: false` for a page that starts with only its own grants, `getPagePlace` tells a page's workspace, space and parent and whether it, a page above it or its space is in Trash, and `getPersonalSpaceId(tx, workspaceId, userId)` is `getPrivateSpaceId` with the workspace first. For History, `getPageLook` reads a page's title, icon, cover and style and `setPageLook` puts them back as one change (403 on a locked page, 409 on an archived one, 404 in Trash); after the commit the caller emits `page.updated` with the keys `setPageLook` returns and calls `nudgePage`, which refreshes open pages and sidebars.
  14. **The tree** (pages 3b): `GET /api/spaces/:id/tree` is built on the server for each person: their own Private section, a team space they're in, or any team space for Admins. It lists the pages and databases they can open, depth first by `position`. Rows, inline databases and what's under them are never in it (SB-10). A page they can open under one they can't comes last at the top, marked `pathHidden`, without that page's ID or title (SB-06). `listSubtree(db, rootId)` returns a page and everything under it that isn't in Trash, parents first, for export, duplicate and history (no access check).
  15. **Moving** (pages 3b): `POST /api/pages/:id/move` needs Full access on the page and Can edit where it goes: Can edit on the parent (Can edit content on a database), or a level of at least Can edit at the top of the space, with the same rules as a new page. Everything under the page moves along, Trash included; a locked page can still be moved (its lock keeps its content and look). Moves in a workspace take turns (an advisory lock, taken before any check), so two moves can't make a loop, and a move that would nest pages more than `MAX_DEPTH` levels deep is refused (400). A page moved into a database becomes a row and a row moved out becomes a page, with `page.kindChanged` after the commit; rows don't move between databases. To another space, the old space must allow moving pages out, and the person needs Full access on the page and on every page under it that keeps its own access (403 otherwise), so nobody opens a closed page by moving it into their Private section. When the move changes who can open the page or a page under it that keeps its own access (grants, members, Admins and the Private section's owner; row rules aren't counted), it's refused with 409 and `details: { losing, gaining }`, counted in people, until it's sent again with `confirmAccessChange` (SB-04). `checkedMove` runs all of this for other callers, such as Trash's "Move to".
  16. **Live nudges** (pages 3b): `space:<id>` (whoever may see the tree) gets `tree-changed` after a page is added, renamed, given an icon or moved; `page:<id>` (whoever can open it) gets `page-changed` after any change to the page. They carry IDs only; browsers refetch. People who lose access are checked again at once.
  17. **Archive** (pages 3b-2; Page history and Trash 3.5): `POST /api/pages/:id/archive` and `POST /api/pages/:id/unarchive` (Full access; `archivePage` and `unarchivePage` check it themselves, because an archived page reads as Can view) archive a page or database with everything under it, Trash included, as one archive (`archive_root_id`); sub-pages archived earlier keep their own, and a row can't be archived alone. Both answer with the page and `subPages`, how many pages under it went along; in an archived space they answer the space's 409. Archived pages are read-only for everyone (Unarchive aside) and leave the tree; links and cards still open them. They take no new sub-pages or moves (409 "This page is archived. Unarchive it first.", checked in `insertPage` and `movePage` too, so other lanes' rows can't land in an archive). Unarchive from any page of an archive brings back all of it (409 while the page above it is archived too). `page.archived` and `page.unarchived` list the pages, and each page also gets `page.updated` with `changes: ['archived']`, so open editors check access again. `getArchivedPageIds(db, pageIds)` tells other lanes (search, Forms, notices) which pages are archived.
- **Consequences:** One place decides access, so a fix there fixes every feature. A person's level is always computed when asked, never stored, so a change to a role, a grant, a space's members or its default access takes effect at once. Groups, share links and access requests add to these rules in later pull requests without changing the functions above.
