# 0005: Spaces, pages and who can open them

- **Status:** accepted (2026-10-27). Core lane, page model. Names are in [0007](0007-shared-contracts.md) (sections 3.3 and 4.1); the rules come from the Pages and blocks and the Roles and permissions specs (RP-1 to RP-26).
- **Context:** Pages, databases and rows share one tree. Routes, live co-editing, comments, search, notices and the other lanes all need the same answer to "what may this person do here?", and none of them may show a title or content to someone without access.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Tables** (`*_pages_spaces_access.sql`): `spaces` (team spaces, and one private space per person and workspace), `pages` (0007 section 4.1, plus `cover_position`, `style`, `locked_*`, `archived_*` and `trash_root_id`), `access_grants` and `access_history`. A page's parent is always in the same space (a composite foreign key), and deleting a page for good deletes everything under it.
  2. **Levels:** `none < view < comment < edit_content < edit < full`. A grant may also be `rows_only`. `edit_content` and `rows_only` are given only on databases.
  3. **A person's level on a page**, worked out in one query for any number of pages:
     - **Private space:** its owner has `full`; nobody else has anything, admins included (RP-D1), unless a page in it is shared with them.
     - **Team space:** admins have `full`. Everyone else gets the highest grant that reaches the page: on the page itself, on each parent while the page below still inherits, and on the space when the top page inherits. A grant reaches a person directly, through their role, through a group, or as everyone in the workspace.
     - **Below a database**, `edit_content` counts as `edit` and `rows_only` as `none`; on the database itself `rows_only` is `view`. Then the Databases lane's `row` resolver may change a row's level; its `ctx.grantLevel` is the best grant that reached the database, as given, so it can open the rows someone is in.
     - **Last, the role limit** from 0007 section 3.3. Guests (`kind = 'guest'`) are reached only by grants to them or to their groups, never by role or everyone grants, and get at most `edit`.
  4. **Changing access:** `grantAccess` and `revokeAccess` need `full` where the grant is, which also means nobody grants above their own level. Private spaces get no space grants; guests get no space grants and never `full`; group grants are refused until groups exist. Every change adds an `access_history` row in the same transaction.
  5. **Answers:** 404 "This was deleted, or the link is wrong." for an ID that is missing, malformed or in Trash; 403 "You don't have access to this." for anything else that's closed, never with a title. `app.requirePageAccess(min, pageIdOf)` answers this way, and `pageAccessOf(request)` gives the route the level it found.
  6. **Registration:** `features/pages/routes.ts` is wrapped in `fastify-plugin`, so `app.requirePageAccess` works in every feature registered after it. A prefix wouldn't apply to it, so its routes use their full paths (`/api/spaces`, `/api/pages`, `/api/trash`, `/api/favorites`, `/api/recent`) and its line in `app.ts` has no prefix.
  7. **Events:** service functions (`createPage`, `trashPage`, `grantAccess` and the others) never emit, because only the caller knows when its transaction commits. The page model's routes emit `page.*` and `access.changed` after their commit; a feature that calls these functions in its own transaction emits after its own commit.
  8. **Nothing stored is shown without a check** (issue #14): pages store other pages only by ID, titles are looked up for the person viewing, and every list (tree, Trash, favorites, recent, cards) is filtered with `getAccessForPages` when it's read. Stored copies such as Markdown or search text are only served after the same check.
- **Consequences:** One place decides access, so a fix there fixes every feature. A person's level is always computed when asked, never stored, so changing a role or a grant takes effect at once. Groups, share links and access requests add to these rules in later pull requests without changing the functions above.
