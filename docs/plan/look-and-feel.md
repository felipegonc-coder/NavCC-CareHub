# Look and feel

_Copied from the Notion plan (the source of truth) on 2026-10-26. If they differ, Notion wins._

> 🎨 The new Care Hub keeps today's look: the "Gonçalves Hub" style. Calm and Notion-like, with neutral greys and near-black accents. Our own design and name, with no copied names or logos.

## Overall feel

- Like Notion: calm, lots of white space, quick to use.
- The Calendar feels like Notion Calendar, the assistant like Notion AI, and courses look like Articulate Rise.

## Text

- System font: San Francisco on Mac, Segoe UI on Windows, with color emoji.
- Page titles about 36 px bold, body text 16 px, small text 14 px, labels 12 px.
- Page text sits in a column about 768 px wide. Full width uses the whole screen. Databases get a wider area.

## Shapes and spacing

- 8 px corners on buttons, cards, menus and blocks.
- Thin light-grey borders. Soft shadows only on menus and pop-ups.
- Sidebar about 256 px wide, in light grey.

## Logo and icons

- Logo: a near-black rounded square (#191919) with a bold white "C". The same mark is the browser tab icon and appears on every sign-in screen.
- Simple line icons (Lucide style). Pages can have an emoji or a colored icon.

## Readability and access

- Text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1, readable greys and a clear focus outline on everything.
- Works with only a keyboard or a screen reader (WCAG 2.1 AA). Bigger-text option. Respects "reduce motion".

## Phones and tablets

- Every screen has a phone layout. The sidebar becomes a menu, and wide tables and boards scroll inside their own area.

## How we check it

- Builders add screenshots of every new screen in light and dark mode, on a computer and a phone.
